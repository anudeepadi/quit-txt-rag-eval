# 📊 Example Outputs from QuitTxt Protocol Testing System

This file shows example outputs to demonstrate how the protocol-guided AI responds.

## Example 1: Craving Emergency

### User Input:
```
I'm having a really strong craving right now. I don't think I can resist!
```

### With Protocol Context:

**Protocol Sections Used:**
- Craving Management Strategies
- Immediate Distraction Techniques
- Support System Activation

**AI Response:**
```
I hear you - cravings can feel overwhelming, but remember they typically only last 
3-5 minutes. Right now, try the 4 D's: Delay (wait 10 minutes), Distract 
(do something with your hands), Deep breathe (take 5 slow breaths), and Drink water. 
You've got this - the craving will pass, and you're stronger than you think!
```

### Without Protocol Context:

**AI Response:**
```
Cravings can be difficult, but try to distract yourself. Maybe go for a walk 
or do something else. Remember why you wanted to quit. You can do it!
```

**Comparison:**
- ✅ With Protocol: Specific, actionable (4 D's technique), time-bound (3-5 minutes)
- ❌ Without Protocol: Generic, less actionable, vague suggestions

## Protocol Context Examples

Here's what actual protocol sections look like when sent to the AI:

```
=== RELEVANT PROTOCOL SECTIONS ===

## Craving Management
When a participant reports a craving, immediately provide the 4 D's technique:
- Delay: Wait 10 minutes before making any decision
- Distract: Do something with your hands (squeeze a stress ball, doodle)
- Deep breathe: Take 5 slow, deep breaths
- Drink water: Hydrate and give your mouth something to do

Remind them that cravings typically last only 3-5 minutes. Each time they 
successfully resist a craving, they're rewiring their brain and making the 
next craving easier to handle...

## Support System Activation
Encourage the participant to reach out to their support system when facing 
a craving or difficult moment. This could be:
- Texting a friend or family member
- Calling their quit buddy
- Posting in their support group
- Messaging you (the counselor) for support...

## Positive Reinforcement
Celebrate every moment the participant successfully resists a craving. 
Acknowledge their strength and remind them that they're one step closer 
to their goal. Each successful resistance builds their confidence and 
makes future cravings easier to manage...
```

This context gets combined with:
- System instructions (be compassionate, supportive, etc.)
- Conversation history (last 5 messages)
- Current user message

To create a comprehensive prompt that guides the AI to respond in a 
protocol-aligned, evidence-based, and supportive manner.
